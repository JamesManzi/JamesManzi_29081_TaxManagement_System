# Tax Management System (TMS)

## Project Overview

The Tax Management System (TMS) is a database project designed to help small businesses in Rwanda manage and keep track of their tax obligations. The system aims to make it easier for business owners to monitor taxes, record payments, check outstanding balances and keep track of important tax deadlines.

The project uses Oracle Database and PL/SQL to manage tax-related information and business rules. It also plans to include a Python Streamlit dashboard connected to the Oracle database, allowing users to view tax information and monitor their payment status.

For the research phase, the project uses data from the **Rwanda Establishment Census 2023**, published by the National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda (NISR), to understand business characteristics and tax-related information.

The main goal is to provide a simple and organised way for small businesses to manage their tax information and improve their awareness of tax responsibilities.

## Technologies

- Oracle Database 26ai
- PL/SQL
- Python
- Streamlit

## Data Source

National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda (NISR) – [Rwanda Establishment Census 2023](https://microdata.statistics.gov.rw/index.php/catalog/112/datafile/F4)

*Note: The census data is used for research and business context. Any sample tax payments or obligations created for system testing will be clearly identified as demonstration data.*
